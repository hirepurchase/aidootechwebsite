# Photography — source

**These are Aidoo Tech's own photographs**, taken in the Kwabenya and Circle
showrooms in October 2026 and supplied by the business. No stock photography,
no licensing to track, and every device shown is stock the shop actually held
on the day.

| Folder | Used on |
| --- | --- |
| `gallery/` | The "On our shelves right now" grid on /products |
| `products/` | The three category cards and the "In the store" band on /products |
| `brands/` | The four brand chips on /products |

Supplied originals: the showroom interior, the Infinix shelf, two Samsung
display cases, the Samsung/Tecno wall, the Tecno shelf, and two cartons of
sealed Galaxy A17 handsets.

## Replacing them

Stock turns over, so these date. Drop new photographs in at the same paths and
nothing else needs changing — the components reference them by filename. If a
model listed in `PHONE_BRANDS` (`src/lib/content.ts`) stops being stocked,
update the note there at the same time so the shelves and the text agree.
