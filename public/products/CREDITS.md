## How these were chosen

Each image was checked before use so the site does not misrepresent the
business:

- **Only brands Aidoo Tech actually stocks are visible.** The two phone
  photographs were cropped down to the Samsung and Infinix handsets; the vivo,
  ASUS and realme devices in the original frames are out of shot. The tablet is
  a Samsung Galaxy Tab.
- **No Apple products.** Most stock photography of phones is of iPhones, and
  Aidoo Tech does not sell them. Candidates showing Apple hardware, Apple
  logos or Apple Store interiors were rejected — the Pexels Licence also
  forbids implying brand endorsement.
- **No foreign-language screens or other shops.** Candidates with Russian
  system UI, Turkish shop signage or another retailer's storefront were
  rejected.

## Replacing these

These are stand-ins for photographs of your own stock. When you photograph
handsets on your own shelves, drop the files in here and update `photo` and
`photoAlt` on each entry in `productCategories` in `src/lib/content.ts`. Your
own stock is always the better picture, and the brand-accuracy checks above
stop mattering once the phones in the photograph are literally yours.
